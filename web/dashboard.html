Why Circuit Saver shifts, not cuts

Circuit Saver's shed/restore mechanism primarily changes when your energy is used, not how much. The economic case doesn't depend on lowering your total kWh — it depends on flattening the narrow peak-demand window that would otherwise justify building an expensive new power plant.

A peaker plant is sized for the top 5% of demand hours in a year. If enough households shift load out of that narrow window, the plant is never needed — regardless of total annual consumption. Secondary savings also come from avoiding rebound-spike inefficiency and shifting consumption toward hours with a cleaner generation mix.
