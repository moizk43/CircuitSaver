The system

Three components, one intervention.

Machine learning predicts what would have happened. A deterministic, auditable optimizer decides what happens instead. Nothing is a black box to the household or the utility.

01

Predict

A Random Forest Regressor, trained separately per region, estimates each household's normal load — the baseline every savings figure is measured against.

02

Allocate

The Swarm Engine — a deterministic, weighted water-filling optimizer, not a model — distributes the shed target across households by fairness, priority, and available capacity.

03

Protect

An Anti-Rebound Classifier staggers restart timing per household, preventing every shed device from switching back on at once and causing a second spike.

04

Audit

Every shed event, allocation, and restart is logged to a ledger — because a household or utility partner deserves to know exactly why their power was shed.
